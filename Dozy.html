Final Project Proposal!

Sleep Bedside Assistant Device

Project Concept / Motivation

I want to build a sleep bedside automated device that helps improve sleep comfort. It will monitor the surrounding temperature and humidity and display this information on a LCD screen. If it gets too warm, a fan will automatically turn on to cool the space, while an RGB LED provides visual feedback of the current temperature.

Anticipated Bill of Materials

  • Arduino Uno (x1)
  • DHT11 sensor (temperature + humidity)
  • 16x2 LCD Display
  • RGB LED
  • DC Fan (5V or 12V)
  • Breadboard and assorted resistors

Timeline and Contingency Plans

  • Investigate optimal temperature and humidity ranges for healthy sleep.
  • Figure out how to connect the DHT sensor, fan, RGB LED, and LCD display.
  • Program the Arduino to read sensor inputs, display real-time temperature and humidity on the LCD, and provide automatic fan control and LED feedback.
  • Test individual components first (LCD, fan, LED) before integrating.
  • Build a desk stand or enclosure to hold the device securely and maintain proper airflow for the fan.
  • Test the final product.
